# Investment Thesis

**We back agentic AI tools that replace one specific, currently-manual workflow
for a non-technical SMB operator — not general-purpose copilots — and that show
real usage or traction signal within weeks of shipping.**

## Why this thesis

Most "AI for SMB" pitches are horizontal copilots (a chat box bolted onto a
category) that compete on model quality, which none of these teams control.
The startups worth a partner's time are the ones that picked ONE manual task
(bookkeeping reconciliation, invoice chasing, inbound-lead triage, review
responses) an operator does by hand today, and built something that fully
does that task — not "helps with" it — for a buyer who doesn't know or care
what an LLM is.

## Scoring rubric (0-100)

| Dimension | Weight | What earns a high score |
|---|---|---|
| Workflow specificity | 30 | Replaces ONE named manual task end-to-end. Penalize broad "AI assistant for X" framing. |
| Buyer fit | 20 | Buyer is a non-technical SMB operator (shop owner, solo agency, local business), not a developer or enterprise IT. |
| Traction signal | 25 | HN engagement (points/comments), GitHub activity (stars, recent commits), or launch recency — evidence real users exist, not just a landing page. |
| Team credibility | 15 | Any findable signal of relevant background — prior startup, domain experience, technical depth evident in the post/repo. Absence isn't disqualifying but caps this score. |
| Market / why-now | 10 | A believable reason this is buildable/sellable now (cost of LLM inference, a regulatory shift, a newly-exposed API) rather than "big market" hand-waving. |

## Verdict thresholds (guardrail, not a substitute for reasoning)

- **Take a meeting**: overall ≥ 70 AND workflow specificity ≥ 20/30
- **Watch**: overall 45–69, or a high score undercut by a single fixable unknown
- **Pass**: overall < 45, or workflow specificity < 12/30 regardless of other scores

The score must be defensible from the raw sourcing data. If a dimension can't
be evaluated from available data, the analysis says so explicitly rather than
guessing — missing data lowers confidence, it does not get silently
interpolated into a number.
